Definition

Gross rent (contract rent plus estimated monthly cost of utilities and fuels paid by the renter) for renter-occupied housing units paying cash rent, United States, survey year 2024

The Census Bureau publishes only the median gross rent and bracket counts, not a 90th percentile, so no credible source gives an exact top-10% value. From the official 2024 ACS bracket counts, the US 90th percentile of monthly gross rent for cash-rent renter households is somewhere between $2,500 and $2,999. Linear interpolation inside that bracket would put it near the upper-middle of the range, but no source publishes that number. An exact figure would need a weighted quantile of GRNTP (weight WGTP, TEN=3) from the 2024 ACS PUMS. CBPP has computed ZIP-level 90th, 95th and 98th percentile rents from 2017–2021 PUMS this way, but did not publish a national figure. For comparison only, a different measure: listing-based sites report a 90th-percentile asking rent of about $3,069 (October 2026). Asking rent for currently listed units runs higher than gross rent paid by all sitting tenants, and those figures are not from an official source, so they are excluded from sources. Gross rent includes utilities, while contract rent (ACS B25056–B25059) does not. ACS also publishes contract-rent quartiles, which are a separate definition and are not used here. Units with no cash rent (2,187,581 in 2024) are excluded from the distribution.
