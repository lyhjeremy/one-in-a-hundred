Definition

Words per minute on a transcription task of 15 English sentences in an online study hosted with a commercial typing-test site. WPM = (characters in the transcribed string / 5) / minutes from first to last keypress, averaged over sentences. Participants self-selected; 12% excluded for uncorrected error rate above 25% or technical problems. Sample mostly young (mean age 24.5, 75% aged 11-30), 68% from the US, 85% native English speakers, 72% had taken a typing course.

No source publishes an explicit 99th-percentile typing speed. The best academic source (Dhakal et al. 2018, n=168,960) gives mean 51.56 WPM, SD 20.2, and a 90th percentile of about 78 WPM, with the fastest typists at 120+ WPM. Platform data place the top-1% cutoff between roughly 80 WPM (Ratatype, Fastfingers, per-test, large share of beginners and non-English tests) and about 100 WPM (TypeLit, per-user, 1.3% exceed 100). All samples are self-selected online typing-test or typing-practice users, skewed young and towards people interested in typing, not the general population. Per-test and per-user figures differ, test length and error handling vary by site, and the question gives no country, sex or age, so world/all is assumed.
