Definition

Family net worth (gross assets minus liabilities, including retirement accounts), families grouped by age of the reference person 65-74, United States, 2025 SCF wave

The question asks about age 65. The SCF reports a 65-74 band, not single years of age. Census SIPP has a closer band, but its wealth definition leaves out equity in pension plans and home furnishings, and it counts households, not SCF family units, so it is listed here only for comparison: the Census Bureau's Wealth of Households: 2024 (P70BR-218, July 2026, 2025 SIPP, https://www2.census.gov/library/publications/2026/demo/p70br-218.pdf) gives a median household wealth of $376,100 for householders aged 65-69 and $426,700 for 70-74 (2024 dollars), against $204,900 for all households. Its p90 for all households is $1,810,000. The SCF counts the 'primary economic unit' (family) rather than every household member, and its list sample oversamples wealthy families while excluding the Forbes 400. The 2022 and 2025 SCF waves are in different dollar years, so they should be inflation-adjusted before averaging. In 2025 dollars the 2022 median is 448,600.
