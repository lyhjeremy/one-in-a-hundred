Definition

Self-reported number of books read in whole or in part in the previous 12 months, any format (print, e-book, audiobook), U.S. adults 18+, phone survey (cell and landline)

Neither source publishes a 99th-percentile value. Both give only the median (5 books, Pew 2021 and Gallup 2005) and the mean (about 12.6 to 14.2). Gallup's banded tables put about 6-8% of U.S. adults at 51 or more books a year (6% in 2005, 8% in 2001 and 2002), so the top 1% threshold is somewhere above 51 books/year. Neither source states the exact figure, and this research does not estimate it. All figures are self-reported recall, not measured. They count books read 'all or part of the way through' and include e-books and audiobooks, so they will overstate completed books. Gallup and Pew are independent surveys with their own samples. The Statista chart and Book Riot article that turn up in searches only repeat Gallup and Pew, so they were not used. The Gallup 2021 PDF with the full 2021 distribution (news.gallup.com/file/poll/388568/220110Books.pdf) could not be parsed here. It may contain a 2021 51+ share.
