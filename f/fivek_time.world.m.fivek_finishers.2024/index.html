Definition

Chip or gun finish time of male finishers in timed road 5K races, all ages pooled; percentile table where the 1st percentile is the fastest 1%. Dataset described as '35 million results collected in the last 20 years from more than 28,000 races'; races and countries not named, methodology not described.

No national statistics office, peer-reviewed paper or parkrun publication gives a men's 5K percentile table; the top-1% figure rests on RunRepeat's aggregated race-result pool, which does not name its races, countries or method. RunRepeat's two tables put the fastest 1% of male 5K race finishers at 17:30 (global calculator, updated 2024) and 17:49 (US 2009-2018 page), and both derive from the same organisation. The population is self-selected timed race finishers, all ages pooled, not men in general; the 2024 US sample from Outside Run says fewer than 2% of all finishers broke 20:00, which is consistent with a men's top-1% mark in the 17-18 minute range. parkrun data is excluded from RunRepeat's US figures. The question gives no country, so the global calculator table is the primary match.
