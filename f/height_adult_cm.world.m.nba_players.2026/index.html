Definition

Official NBA listed height, without shoes (the league has required barefoot measurements since 2019-20), for players on 2025-26 rosters. Gives the ten tallest players, not the full distribution.

Neither source is independent: both rest on the NBA's own listed heights, through SI and Basketball-Reference. No official NBA publication or peer-reviewed paper gives height percentiles for the whole league. The p99 is a rank cutoff on a small population of roughly 550 players, so it amounts to the sixth or so tallest player, and it moves by a full inch depending on who is counted (two-way and G League call-ups such as Zikarsky). Wembanyama's height is disputed: some reports say the Spurs listed him at 7'5" for 2025-26. Comparison on a different population: a peer-reviewed summary of ten years of NBA Draft Combine data (PMC10593457) gives 197.7 ± 7.6 cm, range 181.6-212.7 cm. That covers draft prospects, not league players. The suggested value is about 218 cm (7'2"), with 221 cm (7'3") as the upper bound.
