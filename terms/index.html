What this is

One in a Hundred is a free, personal project. It shows percentile thresholds derived from published statistics, with a grade that says how much the underlying sources agree. It is for curiosity, not for decisions. Nothing here is financial, medical or professional advice.

The numbers can be wrong

Every number traces to a named source, and the grade and range describe how confident we are, but sources contain errors, definitions differ, and the derivation can be mistaken. Facts marked "unreviewed" were produced by an automated research step and have not been checked by a person. Use any figure here as a starting point, and follow the source links before relying on it.
