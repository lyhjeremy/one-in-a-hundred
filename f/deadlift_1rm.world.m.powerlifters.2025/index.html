Definition

Best competition deadlift in kg for male lifters at raw (unequipped), drug-tested, sanctioned full-power (squat-bench-deadlift) meets held 1 Jan to 20 Dec 2025, ages 16-65, one best result per lifter. All bodyweights pooled. Percentiles computed by Strength Origins from OpenPowerlifting.

No source I found publishes a 99th-percentile deadlift for all men across bodyweights. The sources above stop at p90 (288 kg, Strength Origins) or give only the median. The only p99 figures are per weight class: Fitness Volt (fitnessvolt.com/strength-standards/deadlift/) computes raw competition percentiles from OpenPowerlifting, tested and untested pooled, date range not stated, refreshed June 2026. Its male p99 values are 287.5 kg at 74 kg, 305 kg at 83 kg (n 80,506), 320 kg at 93 kg (n 82,658), 332.5 kg at 105 kg, 347.5 kg at 120 kg and 360 kg at 125 kg. Its p50 values are 217.5 kg at 83 kg and 230 kg at 93 kg, and p90 is 265 kg at 83 kg and 280 kg at 93 kg. Because these are per class, they are left out of sources. The peer-reviewed source, van den Hoek et al. 2024 (J Sci Med Sport 27(10):734-742, doi 10.1016/j.jsams.2024.07.005, 571,650 male drug-tested raw entries), reports deadlift as a multiple of bodyweight and stops at the 90th decile: 3.25x bodyweight for men aged 18-35. That is a different scale, so it is not in sources either. Strength Level (strengthlevel.com, self-reported estimated 1RMs from app users, 5,398,185 male results, 2015-2026) gives a different, weaker distribution: p50 154 kg, p80 202 kg, p95 253 kg, with no p99. Symmetric Strength gym data via Fitness Volt (self-reported, n 376,573 men) gives p50 147.2 kg and p90 204 kg. Every competition source draws on the same OpenPowerlifting database, so none is independent of the others. They describe competitive raw powerlifters, a far stronger group than men in general. The question asks about men without qualification, and no measured general-population deadlift data exists.
