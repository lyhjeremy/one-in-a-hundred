Definition

Finish time of half marathon finishers, all ages pooled, in a percentile table where the 1st percentile is the fastest 1%. Dataset described as about 35 million results from more than 28,000 races collected over the last 20 years, across all distances; races, countries and timing method (chip vs gun) not named; methodology not described. Author Vania Nikolova.

The only source that publishes a top 1% (fastest 1%) threshold for a large multi-race pool is RunRepeat (1:23:59 all finishers, 1:18:37 men, 1:35:55 women), and its methodology, race mix, countries and exact years are undisclosed; the pool spans about 20 years rather than one reference year and is likely US-heavy, so it is not a true world population. The 2024 US compilation reported by Outside stops at the top 5% and gives a slower overall median (about 2:13 vs 2:14:59), and a single race (San Francisco) puts its fastest 1% at 1:27:52, so a top 1% near 1:24-1:28 for mixed-sex finishers is consistent across sources. No national statistics office or peer-reviewed study publishes half marathon finish-time percentiles for all finishers; the question did not specify sex, country or age, so the combined-sex, all-ages figure is the match.
