Definition

Number of correct push-ups completed in 2 minutes on the APFT; 5,346 male and 676 female soldiers at 14 US installations, age-banded as the APFT standards are. Percentile tables run from the 1st to the 99th percentile per age band.

No national statistics office or population survey publishes a 99th percentile for standard push-ups. The only official source with a p99 is the US Army's 1988 APFT normative study (male soldiers, 2-minute test): about 83-84 push-ups for men under 37, falling to 70 at ages 42-46. Cooper Institute norms (1-minute test, clinic and law-enforcement clientele) give a p99 of 100 for men 20-29 and 86 for 30-39, but the Cooper tables were only reachable through a sheriff's office reproduction. Protocols differ (1 minute, 2 minutes, untimed, cadence-capped) and are not interchangeable, and every source is a gated or self-selected sample fitter than the general male population. If shipped, label the fact as 'US Army male soldiers, 2-minute test, 1988' rather than 'men'.
