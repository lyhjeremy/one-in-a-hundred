Definition

Device-measured nightly sleep duration: duration of the sleep period time window (longest sleep block in each noon-to-noon day) minus summed wake bouts, from an ActiGraph GT3X+ worn on the wrist for 7 days; participants needed at least 3 valid days; nights under 3 h or over 15 h excluded. NHANES 2011-2012 and 2013-2014 cycles, 4-year survey weights.

No source publishes a single median for all adults. The only published medians are device-measured NHANES values by adult age band (7.83 to 8.26 h, 2011-2014); the 46-55 band is shown as representative. Wearable and under-mattress datasets (Samsung, Withings) give means around 7.1 to 7.6 h and are gated on owning the device. Self-reported means are 7.0 to 7.6 h on workdays, and the BLS time-diary average of 9.03 h counts time in bed, naps and wakefulness, so it is not comparable. The question names no country; US data were used because they are the only ones with published percentiles, while the world-level source gives a mean and SD only. All sources are independent of one another.
