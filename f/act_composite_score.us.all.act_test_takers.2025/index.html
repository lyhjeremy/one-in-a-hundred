Definition

ACT Composite score (1–36) of 2025 US high school graduates who took the ACT as sophomores, juniors or seniors under standard or extended time and got a college-reportable composite. Uses each student's most recent test. CP is the cumulative percent of students at or below a score.

The population is students who chose or were required to take the ACT (about 36% of the 2025 US graduating class), not all US 17–18-year-olds. States that test everyone sit alongside self-selected college-bound students in other states. 'Top 1%' can mean two things. 99% of test-takers scored 34 or lower, so p99 = 34. To be inside the top 1% you need a 35 or 36 (0.90% of the 2025 class). Both ACT sources are built from ACT's own records, so they are not independent. The national ranks table pools three graduating classes and rounds to whole percents. Only one score per student is counted, the most recent; superscores are not used. ACT changed the test from 2025 (enhanced ACT: shorter, science optional, composite from English, math and reading). The Class of 2025 composite mostly reflects the legacy four-section format, so later cohorts may not be directly comparable. SAT scores are a different scale and are not included.
