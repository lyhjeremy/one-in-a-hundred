Definition

Span: maximum horizontal distance between the fingertips of both hands with the arms stretched out sideways, measured by trained anthropometrists on minimally clothed US Army soldiers. This is the ANSUR I working sample, ages about 17-51.

Only one source on this definition could be retrieved. The source measures US Army men in 1988, not all men. Army entry rules on height and weight, and the narrow age range (about 17-51), mean the extremes may be cut off compared with civilians, though stature in this sample (mean 175.6 cm) is close to US civilian men. The 2012 follow-up survey (ANSUR II, Gordon et al. 2014, NATICK/TR-15/007; 4,082 men, values in mm) also measured span and would be the more current second source. For comparison only, not on the same basis: a City University of Hong Kong anthropometry compilation lists span for US men aged 19-65 as p5 1670 mm, p50 1810 mm, p95 1950 mm, SD 84 mm, and p95 1925 mm for British men aged 19-65. It cites no primary survey (it appears to be estimated in the style of Pheasant's Bodyspace) and gives no p99. Arm span runs a few cm longer than height in adult men: in this sample the median span is 182.1 cm against a median height of 175.5 cm.
