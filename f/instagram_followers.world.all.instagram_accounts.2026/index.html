Definition

Follower count of Instagram profiles held in Lurk's own cached-profiles store (its live follow-graph), restricted to profiles with a known positive follower count. Percentiles computed exactly (percentile_cont) over all such profiles. Concentration figures rank accounts into 100 equal-count buckets.

No national statistics office, UN agency, Meta disclosure or peer-reviewed paper publishes the follower distribution of all Instagram accounts; Meta does not release it and researchers cannot enumerate users (a 2014 Arizona State crawl of 1 million users, arXiv 1410.8099, publishes only log-log plots, no quantiles). The two usable sources are companies that hold their own account data and both samples are non-random. Lurk's set over-represents searched and tracked accounts and gives p99 of 1,522,437 followers; Mention's set is active posting accounts seen by a brand-monitoring tool, and its brackets place the top 1% boundary between 500,000 and 1,000,000 followers. A true random sample of every registered account, including inactive, private and spam accounts, would have a substantially lower 99th percentile than either figure. Treat any published value as describing visible, active public accounts rather than all Instagram accounts.
