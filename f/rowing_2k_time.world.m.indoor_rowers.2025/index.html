The population is self-selected: men who row on a Concept2 RowErg and choose to log a 2000m piece online. Many are club, masters or former competitive rowers, so these times are much faster than the general male population would manage. 'Top 1%' here means the top 1% of ranked men, not of all men. Unverified results are included. Percentiles come from rank positions, not from a distribution Concept2 publishes itself.
