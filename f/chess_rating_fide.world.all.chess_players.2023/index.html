'Rated players' is ambiguous. The primary answer uses the FIDE standard (over-the-board classical) list, where FIDE's own 2023 analysis states 99% of rated players are below 2400 and the October 2026 active list places the cutoff between 2350 and 2400 (roughly FIDE Master level). That pool is gated: FIDE publishes no rating below 1400 (1000 before March 2024), so it excludes most casual and club players. National federation pools give lower cutoffs (US Chess all members, 2004: 2200-2299 band). Online pools are on different scales: Lichess rapid top 1% is about 2250 and blitz about 2375-2400 among players active that week; chess.com publishes no distribution table. FIDE ratings deflated through the 2010s and were compressed in March 2024, which changed the bottom of the list but not ratings above 2000. No source states an exact top-1% rating; the FIDE figure is a band, and the Lichess and 2026 FIDE cutoffs were located from published counts rather than quoted.
